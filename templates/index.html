How Our Recommendation Algorithm Works

Our system uses Machine Learning and Content-Based Filtering to find movies similar to your selected movie.

📑

Feature Extraction

Combines rich movie attributes including genres, keywords, plot overview, lead cast, and director into a unified textual metadata vector.

🧠

TF-IDF Vectorization

Converts cleaned movie descriptions into term frequency-inverse document frequency matrices, weighting unique keywords above common terms.

📐

Cosine Similarity

Calculates multi-dimensional cosine angles between feature vectors, sorting the catalog to output the top 10 highest-affinity recommendations.
